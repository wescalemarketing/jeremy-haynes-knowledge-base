## Operator questions for further review

- Which buyer question remains unresolved before a booked call?
- Is the bottleneck qualification, educational delivery, sales capacity or economics?
- What measured behavior supports an assumption about consumption?
- Which recommendations concern cold traffic, warm audiences, follow-up or post-purchase activation?
- Which outcomes are demonstrations, forecasts or self-reported cases?

Run `python3 tools/kb.py search "funnel friction capacity" --claims-only` for article evidence plus related video navigation. The [video CSV](video-inventory.csv) contains the full inventory; a title match alone cannot answer a methodology question.
